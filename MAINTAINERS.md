There is no second reviewer, and no family has a specialist owner yet.
That is a real limit. CI checks structure, prose, code, citation liveness,
and internal links. It does not check that a distributed-systems claim, a
security claim, or a category-theory claim is right. Domain review is still
one person.

A contributor becomes a family owner by a sustained run of correct
corrections in that family, after which the maintainer edits
`.github/CODEOWNERS` to name them on that directory. Opening a pull request
does not grant that ownership.

Security reports follow `.github/SECURITY.md`.
